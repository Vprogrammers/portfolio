# Contact form

Name, email and message, with native validation and a hidden spam trap.

- With `accessKey` (a free Web3Forms key), the form posts to Web3Forms and the message arrives in the inbox linked to that key. The form then shows a thank-you, or an error that points to the address.
- Without a key, submitting opens the visitor's mail app with a ready subject and body, addressed to `email`.

Data: `title`, `text`, `email`, `accessKey`, `id` (default `contact-form`).
