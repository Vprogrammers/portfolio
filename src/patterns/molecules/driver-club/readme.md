# Driver club

A members' club for taxi drivers, on a 16 s timeline:
- Face ID signs in.
- The driving licence is captured front and back, with crop handles closing in on each side.
- The yellow member badge is issued and partner offers slide in, over the app's own photo of London cabs (dimmed and softened) and a moving taxi checker band.

Data: `photo`, `faceid`, `licence` (`title`, `front`, `back`), `brand`, `member` (`name`, `id`, `level`), `offers` (`deal`, `partner`, `hue`; three). The parent sets `--club-h`, `--club-x` and `--club-y`.
