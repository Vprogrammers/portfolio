# Duet studio

A short-video studio in duet mode, on a 16 s timeline:
- Two vertical clips play side by side, with dancing silhouettes.
- Filters cycle over them with their names, AR sparkles twinkle and stickers drop in, while the record ring fills.
- The clip then posts: caption, counters and likes climb, and hearts float up.

Data: `filters` (five names), `stickers` (three), `music`, `modes`, `post` (`user`, `caption`, `likes`: four steps, `comments`, `shares`). The parent sets `--duet-h`, `--duet-x` and `--duet-y`.
