# Kids TV

ÇocukFlix on a playful sky (drifting clouds, rising balloons, twinkling stars), on a 16 s timeline. A yellow kids' tablet sways in front, running the app in Turkish:
- the cartoon grid;
- a tap opens the player, where the character hops, the progress runs and the heart saves the video to favourites;
- the gear opens the parental gate: a sum that a grown-up solves, with the answer key pressed;
- the gate unlocks the settings, including a screen-time limit.

The logo letters bob in colour, and the characters are simple drawn blobs, so no real cartoons are shown.

Data:
- `brand`, `tabs`
- `videos` (`title`, `hue`; six; the first is played)
- `saved`
- `gate` (`title`, `sum`, `answer`, `keys`; the third key is the answer)
- `settings` (`title`, `rows`: `label`, `value`)

The parent sets `--tablet-h`, `--tablet-x` and `--tablet-y`.
